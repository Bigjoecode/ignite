Orthodontic Conditions & Solutions

Personalised Orthodontic Treatment in Arlington Heights

Don't let misaligned teeth or jaws affect your oral health and confidence. These are the conditions we correct most often.

Overbite

When the top teeth extend too far over the lower teeth.

Crowding

Overlapping teeth that are difficult to clean properly.

Gapped Teeth

Significant spacing between teeth that can affect the bite.

Crossbite

Also known as an underbite, where the jaws don't align.

Open Bite

When the top and bottom teeth don't meet on closing.
