The Ignite Difference

Orthodontic Care Built Around You.

Choosing an orthodontist is about more than finding someone who can straighten teeth. It’s about finding a team that listens, explains your options clearly, and makes treatment work with your life.

At Ignite Orthodontics, we combine specialist orthodontic care with flexible options designed around our patients. From your first consultation through the final stages of treatment, our goal is to make your experience clear, comfortable, and easier to manage.

Night & Weekend Appointments

Your schedule doesn’t always fit a traditional 9-to-5. We offer convenient appointment times to make orthodontic care easier to fit around work, school, and family.

Flexible Payment Options

Orthodontic treatment is an investment. Flexible payment options can help you spread the cost of care and choose an approach that works with your budget.

Insurance-Friendly Care

We work with many dental insurance plans and can help you understand your available orthodontic benefits and expected out-of-pocket costs.
